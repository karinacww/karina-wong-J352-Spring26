Is ChatGPT becoming less popular?

New findings on AI model usage

By Karina

Apr 16, 2026

New data shows ChatGPT’s share of AI platform web traffic has dropped sharply over the past year, signaling a shift in the competitive landscape as new competitors join the market. Its share fell from about 87% in January 2025 to roughly 64% in January 2026, as rival platforms gained traction and drew in more users.
